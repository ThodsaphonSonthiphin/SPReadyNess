What changed in rev 2

  • Now Score added (screen 03) — live recompute before a session, per ADR 0010. Marked NOW with a timestamp and a dashed arc so it is never confused with the Morning Score.
  • Three history screens deferred per ADR 0011, superseding ADR 0007. The Capture and the 120-record store are unchanged.
  • The evening drop is shown honestly. Screens 02 and 03 are the same day: 84 in the morning, 68 at 18:04. Body Battery fell 88 → 45 by the clock while Recovery actually improved 73 → 92. That gap is the cost of a live recompute.
  • Still unverified — background memory ceiling, Application.Storage limit, and the averaging window behind averageRestingHeartRate. To be checked against the SDK, not assumed.
